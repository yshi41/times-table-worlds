How to play

  • The run: you race down three lanes. Each gate shows the problem; steer into the lane with the right answer and grab the treats in between. ← → or A D on a keyboard, swipe or tap left/right on a touch screen.
  • A wrong gate costs a heart. Streaks earn bonus treats and Glow Mode for double points. The run speeds up as you get answers right and eases off when you miss.
  • The boss: after 9 gates the Grumpy Storm Cloud attacks. Pick the right answer (1-4, arrows + Enter, or tap) to strike back. Faster answers hit harder, 3 in a row charges a special move, a wrong answer means a lightning strike.
  • Beat the boss for stars and a tougher boss next time. Every 3 stars adds something new to your world.
  • Sibling Showdown: two kids, one screen, same problem. First right answer takes the point. Keyboard: W A D S on the left, arrow keys on the right.
